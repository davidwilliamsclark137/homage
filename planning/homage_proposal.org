* Purpose

The fabric of societal relationships is currently having challenges. The uniqueness of this application is that we propose an alternative solution to connecting people in the data age. We would like to enrich the lives of our users by facilitating ways to use the technology as a means of connecting in real life as opposed to having the technology using us as a means of connection.

* Problem

This is an entirely ambitious project that aims to be created by one individual with access to a 200 dollar codex account. It is important for the piece to be oriented in such a way that it is fun, cool, and inspiring. The main means of AI has been to increase productivity. While these are all good ideas to have, it is believed that utilizing art and technology to blow the dust off of life is going to be absolutely necessary for culture to move in a positive direction.

* Working Questions

** Product

- Who is the first real user of Document Studio?
- What document should someone be able to make in under five minutes?
- What should feel magical on a phone instead of merely possible?
- What should be saved locally even when network access is unavailable?

** Writing flow

- What are the minimum fields needed to publish a useful document?
- Should the app begin with a blank page, a guided prompt, or a reusable template?
- How much LaTeX control should be exposed to the user?
- What does "finished" mean: PDF, artifact, local library entry, shared drop, or all of these?

** Technical

- Which document metadata belongs in SQLite?
- Which generated files belong on disk?
- What should the backend generate versus what the client can preview locally?
- What needs to work when the phone is remote over an Expo tunnel?
